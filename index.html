1. Morphology-Aware Channel-Decoupled Mamba Network (MCDNet) for Medical Image Segmentation

Primary Research Contributor  |  Manuscript in preparation
  • Research contribution: Led literature review, problem formulation, architecture design, experimental evaluation, and result analysis.
  • Network architecture: Proposed a U-shaped Mamba network integrating morphology-aware local feature enhancement with channel-decoupled state-space modelling.
  • Methodological development: Designed the MCSS block for grouped channel processing and adaptive integration; developed MAEM using deformable convolutions and channel attention to adapt local feature extraction to anatomical variations.
